## The shift in commercial intake

Across the U.S. insurance ecosystem, from mid-market carriers to large enterprises, AI is changing how submissions move from broker to underwriter. Optical character recognition, language models, and rules-based extraction now handle the long tail of unstructured PDFs, ACORD forms, and email bodies that used to sit in someone's inbox for hours.

## Where AI thrives

- **Document extraction** — pulling line items, named insureds, and effective dates out of attachments at near-human accuracy.
- **Triage** — routing submissions to the right desk based on class of business, premium size, and broker.
- **First-pass risk scoring** — assigning a preliminary appetite verdict so underwriters spend their time on the right files.

## Where AI fails

AI is brittle when the upstream data is wrong. A misread limit on a policy schedule cascades into bad pricing decisions. The right pattern is **human-in-the-loop**: AI proposes, underwriters confirm, and every correction feeds back into the model.

## What we recommend

1. Start narrow — pick one line of business and one document type.
2. Measure precision _and_ recall before scaling.
3. Keep audit trails so compliance can reconstruct any decision.

The teams that succeed treat AI as a co-pilot, not an autopilot.
