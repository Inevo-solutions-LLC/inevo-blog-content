Commercial insurers are investing heavily in AI. Claims are being processed faster. Underwriters have more capacity. Manual tasks are disappearing.

On paper, that sounds like success.

But there’s a question many organizations still struggle to answer:

**How much value is AI actually creating?**

It’s easy to measure what changed after an AI solution was introduced. Processing time went down. Productivity went up. Fewer people were needed for certain tasks.

But without a clear baseline, those numbers don’t tell the whole story.

Maybe the process was already improving. Maybe the real bottleneck sits somewhere else. Or maybe the efficiency gains are real—but the financial impact is much smaller than expected.

That’s where the difference between an **AI pilot** and an **AI business case** becomes critical.

To understand the ROI of AI, insurers need to look beyond efficiency metrics and connect operational improvements to measurable business outcomes.

**What should you measure? Where does the value actually come from? And how do you build a baseline that makes the ROI impossible to hand-wave away?**

We explore these questions in _The ROI of AI in Commercial Insurance_—a practical look at how insurers can move from promising AI pilots to measurable, defensible business value.
